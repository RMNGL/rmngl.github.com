RMNGL (Remingle) is a holding index for products and platforms released in public order — #1, #2, and the slots that follow.

Each entry is tagged with a content type, platform, and year. Published work links out; unreleased slots stay in the index until they are ready. The name is the practice: separate things brought back into a shared field.

This site is the catalogue. New releases are added to the index as they ship; the archive is the product.
